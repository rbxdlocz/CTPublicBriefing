THE NEXT ERA
A Message to the Faction

Phase II is over. The enforcement era has ended — not in defeat, but in victory. What comes next is the decision that defines CT's future. This page exists to make sure every member understands what is being considered and why.

NOTE
An announcement was recently posted by Chairman Potato outlining Phase III — CT Boot Camp, Watch Guard, and Welcoming Center as the faction's new direction. That option is still on the table. This page presents that proposal alongside a second option that has since been developed. Both are being considered. Your thoughts on either will carry weight in the final decision.
Overview
Where CT Stands

Last month NBTF voted to implement official game mechanics to address cross-teaming. The community spoke clearly — and in doing so, they validated everything CT has stood for since day one. The problem we were built to solve is now being handled at the game level.

That is not a loss. That is the culmination of CT's original mission. We fought long enough and loud enough that the game itself had no choice but to act. Phase II ends in success.

The Reality

With the enforcement mission complete, CT faces a question every faction eventually has to answer — what do we stand for when the original fight is over? Two serious options are on the table. Neither is the wrong answer. Both require commitment.

Activity has dipped. That's honest and the council knows it. But the fact that members are still here, still reading this, still reacting to announcements — that means something. There is still a faction worth saving. The question is which direction gives CT the best chance at a real next chapter.

The Two Paths
What CT Becomes

Two distinct directions are being considered. Read both carefully before sharing your thoughts on Discord. Neither is fully finalized — details will be shaped by the council with input from the faction.

Option A
Phase III
The Institution
CT transitions from an enforcement faction into NBTF's premier training and onboarding institution. The focus shifts to welcoming new players, developing members, and becoming the faction that represents what organized, structured gameplay looks like — while everything else in the faction ecosystem breaks down around us.
CT WatchGuards — casual on-call squads who play the game together, help new players they encounter, and keep CT visible and approachable on NBTF servers
CT Welcoming Center — every new CT member gets personally guided through NBTF before anything else. Map knowledge, game mechanics, CT culture. The front door.
CT Boot Camp — remains the core training pipeline. Three modules, structured progression, graduation ceremony. This is where members are made.
Option B
Contested Territory
The Inner War
The Eternal War against cross-teaming ended in CT's greatest victory. With no external enemy left, CT turns inward — not in chaos, but in organized, structured conflict. Ravens and Enforcers, always on opposite sides of NBTF, now face each other in an ongoing war with real stakes, real standings, and real events. CT becomes the faction that IS the competition.
The Inner War — Ravens (facility) vs Enforcers (rebels) in permanent organized conflict. Running standings, seasonal events, faction-wide rivalry with lore and history behind every engagement
Public Integration — regular NBTF players can plug into CT's conflict through a for-hire system and contract requests. CT's war becomes the server's entertainment
CT Boot Camp — still exists as the entry point. New members are trained and then choose their side. Which company you fight for matters more than ever.
Important

Neither option is fully built yet. What you see here is the direction, not the complete system. The council will develop whichever path is chosen with input from the faction. Your voice in this matters.
